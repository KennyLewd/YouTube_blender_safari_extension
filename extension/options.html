In the regular (non-theater) view, spreads the picture's colors this far past the player and fades them into the glow, so the player edge disappears like in theater mode. Set to 0 to turn off.

Desktop glow

Motion

Smooth motion blends frames for a softer glow, so it trails the video slightly and runs at the full frame rate. Choosing a frame rate turns it off. Sync video makes the video wait for the glow so they never drift apart, but heavy scenes may drop a frame. The frame rate also applies to edge blending.

Connecting to the player…

Balanced glow softens the surround and lowers its brightness. Match video motion turns off smooth motion and fading, and sets 60 fps. More controls are inside the player’s ambient-light menu.

After switching ambient light on or off above, refresh the desktop page.
